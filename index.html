About Revenge Health

Supportive coaching with a bold, no-excuses feel.

Revenge Health was created for clients who are ready to stop starting over and begin building a healthier lifestyle with structure, compassion, and accountability.

The focus is coaching: helping clients create realistic routines, stay consistent with nutrition goals, track progress, and feel supported throughout their wellness journey, including GLP-2/GLP-3 assistance when the client is already working with a licensed provider.

What clients can expect

  • Private coaching and wellness accountability
  • Meal planning and routine support
  • Check-ins for weight and progress
  • GLP-2/GLP-3 journey assistance for provider-guided clients
  • Encouragement without judgment
  • Clear reminders to keep your primary care provider informed
